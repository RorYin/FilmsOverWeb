Email Automation (Guest Onboarding & Password Reset)

Sends auto-generated credentials to newly invited guests.

Why did you see "[Errno 101] Network is unreachable" on Render?

Render explicitly blocks outbound network traffic on standard SMTP ports (25, 465, and 587) on all free tier accounts to prevent spam. Standard HTTPS on Port 443 is 100% open. We support Resend API below, which connects over HTTPS Port 443 and is never blocked by Render!
