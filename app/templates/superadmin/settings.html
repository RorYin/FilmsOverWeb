Cloudflare Stream Worker (Zero Server Bandwidth)

Routes Google Drive video streams directly to the client browser with 100% free, unlimited bandwidth.

How to deploy your free Cloudflare Worker in 2 minutes:

  1. Log in to dash.cloudflare.com ↗ (free tier has unlimited bandwidth).
  2. Go to Compute (Workers & Pages) → Click Create Application → Create Worker.
  3. Click Deploy, then click Edit code, and paste the code from cloudflare_worker/worker.js.
  4. Click Save and Deploy, copy your worker's public URL, and paste it below.

When configured, all video playback streams directly through Cloudflare, consuming 0 MB of your Render bandwidth.

Google Mail Automation (Guest Onboarding)

Sends auto-generated login credentials to new guests from your configured Gmail address.

How to configure Gmail SMTP with an App Password:

Google blocks normal Gmail account passwords on SMTP. You must generate a dedicated 16-character Google App Password:

  1. Make sure 2-Step Verification is ON for your Google account.
  2. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords ↗
  3. Type "FilmsOverWeb" as app name and click Create.
  4. Copy the 16 characters (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop) and paste below.
